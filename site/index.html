Repositioning ĐỒNG

Brand strategist and marketing lead on moving ĐỒNG from fashion accessory to cultural gifting brand, grounded in field research across 300+ brands.

Culture is a living stream. Heritage lasts only when it leaves the glass cabinet and goes back into everyday life.

The problem

ĐỒNG launched selling silk bandanas to young people as fashion. That meant short product lifecycles, chasing trends, price pressure from cheap imports and thin margins on something buyers saw as casual wear.

The gap

Local pride and state support for cultural industries were rising, yet printed merchandise lacked design and depth, and traditional crafts were bulky, costly and hard to display. Nobody was making cultural gifts that are affordable, used every day, well packaged and true to their story.

Three audiences

In gifting, the person who spreads the story, the buyer and the user are rarely the same, so each gets its own message.

  • Story amplifiers: culturally engaged urban youth, 18–34.
  • Buyers: young millennials and Gen Z, 25–34, looking for a thoughtful gift.
  • Recipients: they care about fabric, durability and what the gift means.

For young urban buyers who want a gift to mean something, ĐỒNG is a cultural gifting brand of useful, heritage-rich objects.

The ferryman scarf

Người Lái Đò, a silk scarf for ĐỒNG drawn from Đông Hồ folk prints: the ferryman is the teacher who carries each class of students across. Top 2 best-seller at ĐỒNG since launch.

From the print to the scarf

The boat moving through the waves is the work of raising the young; the lotus stands for purity, wisdom and what patient teaching grows. ĐỒNG makes cultural gifts as ways to show care and thanks, and this one was made for teachers.

My role: market research, concept development, product marketing and communication.

The ngoại án screen, Huế

Nine zodiac scarves

For ĐỒNG's zodiac collection we took the Thọ cloud motifs of Huế's ngoại án screens, the walls set before Nguyễn-dynasty buildings for protection, and grouped nine animals into their Tam Hợp triads. Each animal carries one blessing.

Born in January or before Tết? Your lunar year is probably the one before.

Fire triad

  • Vững vàng: steady and sure-footed.

  • Phơi phới: buoyant, in high spirits.

  • Son sắt: faithful, through everything.

Wood triad

  • Tròn trịa: whole, nothing missing.

  • An yên: calm and at peace.

  • Ấm êm: warm and gentle at home.

Water triad

  • Mỹ mãn: everything turning out well.

  • Mơn mởn: fresh and growing, like young rice.

  • Hân hoan: joyful.

My role: market research, concept development, product marketing and communication.
